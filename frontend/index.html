❖ 1. Level 1 — File Triage & Fingerprinting

Evidence intake begins with non-destructive preservation. Cryptographic digests (SHA-256, MD5, SHA-1) establish digital custody integrity. Magic header bytes (e.g. \x89PNG\r\n\x1a\n, \xFF\xD8\xFF, %PDF-, PK\x03\x04, MZ) are verified against claimed filename extensions to detect file disguise attacks. Global Shannon entropy is computed to identify compressed/encrypted payloads.

❖ 2. Level 2 — Format-Specific Steganography Screening

  • Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) PoV Analysis: Sequential LSB embedding equalizes adjacent pixel color values ($2k, 2k+1$). When observed frequencies become unnaturally identical, the p-value drops below $0.05$, signaling high probability of concealed data.
  • RS Steganalysis (Fridrich et al.): Computes the count of Regular ($R$) and Singular ($S$) pixel blocks under dual flipping masks $M$ and $-M$. In clean images, $R_M \approx R_{-M}$ and $S_M \approx S_{-M}$ with $R_M > S_M$. Stego embedding closes this gap, allowing payload fraction estimation.
  • Container Boundary Analysis: Scans for bytes appended past standard container markers (e.g., beyond JPEG End-of-Image FF D9 or PNG IEND).
  • Unicode Zero-Width Detector: Uncovers invisible covert channels embedded via zero-width spaces (\u200B, \u200C, \u200D, \uFEFF) and reconstructs the decoded ASCII payload.
  • Document & Executable Inspection: Detects project-specific /StegoData PDF dictionaries, word/custom/stegodata.xml parts, and PE section header overlay bytes.

❖ 3. Level 3 — Forensic Correlation Engine

Correlates independent indicators into a consolidated Risk Index (0–100). Files are categorized as CLEAN, LOW RISK, SUSPICIOUS, or HIGH SUSPICION. False-positive safeguards prevent single minor anomalies from falsely branding evidence as tampered.

⚠️ Evidentiary Limitations & Responsible Use

This system is an automated triage aid designed to prioritize files for deeper review. The presence of an anomaly is not definitive proof of malicious activity, and a clean result does not guarantee absence of sophisticated concealment. Final forensic conclusions must be verified with validated forensic suites and qualified examiner review.
